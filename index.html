VISION FOR PUNJAB

Four Core Pillars of Mission 2027

Our commitment to rebuilding a prosperous, peaceful, and self-reliant Punjab under the leadership of Sukhpal Singh Khaira.

Farmers & Agriculture

Ensuring guaranteed MSP, agrarian debt relief, crop diversification support, groundwater preservation, and dignity for farm workers.

  • Fair MSP for all major crops
  • Water table revival plan
  • Agricultural debt waiver

Youth & Employment

Establishing regional industrial hubs, skill development centers, stopping youth brain-drain migration, and promoting sports.

  • Local job creation hubs
  • Modern IT & Skill Parks
  • Sports infrastructure in villages

Transparent Governance

Eradicating administrative corruption, breaking drug cartel networks, enforcing rule of law, and ensuring public accountability.

  • Zero-tolerance for corruption
  • Strict action against drug trade
  • Public service guarantee act

Education & Health

Upgrading government schools with digital infrastructure, building affordable super-specialty hospitals, and empowering women.

  • Smart government schools
  • Affordable healthcare for all
  • Women welfare schemes

ABOUT THE LEADER

A Fearless Voice for Punjab

Sukhpal Singh Khaira has consistently stood up for truth, transparency, and public welfare across Punjab.

As a senior political leader and MLA from Bholath, he has led pivotal legislative debates, raised issues of farmer distress, advocated for clean water and environment, and exposed administrative irregularities with unyielding courage.

  • Elected MLA representing Bholath Assembly Constituency, Punjab
  • Former Leader of Opposition in the Punjab Legislative Assembly
  • Prominent leader in Indian National Congress (INC Punjab)
  • Active participant in national agrarian movements & MSP protections
  • Advocate for canal water access to every agricultural field in Punjab
  • Opponent of land acquisition exploitation and corporate dominance
  • Facilitated major rural road & bridge connectivity projects in Bholath
  • Upgraded civil health infrastructure and flood protection embankments
  • Accessible 24/7 public grievance listening office for residents
